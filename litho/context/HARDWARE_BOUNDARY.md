# Hardware boundary (human-maintained canon)

The planned body is purchased from suppliers in China (PRC), not developed in these repositories.
The candidate mobile base, arm/manipulator and camera are **External** hardware.
Unitree is a possible supplier, not a selected or implemented in-repository platform.
IP scope: perception + SOP/evidence policy + CAD bridge + orchestration/HITL.
A camera interface or robot-command schema does not establish a working manipulator driver.
Robot drivers, motion control, safety interlocks and physical deployment remain External / not implemented as an integrated in-repo product.
The Flex factory line, Technion lab and Israel National Physical AI testbed are **External target environments**.
Naming these environments does not claim a partnership, permission, signed pilot or deployment.
Hardware procurement and integration require separate engineering and evaluation work.
